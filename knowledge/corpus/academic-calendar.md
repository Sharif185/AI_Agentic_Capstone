# Makerere University — Academic Calendar

**Source:** Makerere University official website, https://mak.ac.ug/students/academic-calendar
**Retrieved:** 15 September 2026
**Use:** Reference document for the Student-Support Case Agent's RAG corpus. Content below is a paraphrased summary of the official calendar, not a verbatim reproduction.

## 2026/2027 Academic Year

- Fresher's Orientation Week: 1–7 August 2026 (7 days)
- Semester One: 8 August – 5 December 2026 (17 weeks)
- Semester One Examinations: 16 November – 5 December 2026 (3 weeks)
- Semester One Holiday Break: 5 December 2026 – 15 January 2027 (6 weeks)
- 77th Graduation Week: 11–15 January 2027
- Semester Two: 16 January – 15 May 2027 (17 weeks)
- Semester Two Examinations: 26 April – 15 May 2027 (3 weeks)
- Recess Term: 15 May – 24 July 2027 (10 weeks)

## 2025/2026 Academic Year

- Fresher's Orientation Week: 2–8 August 2025 (7 days)
- Semester One: 9 August – 6 December 2025 (17 weeks)
- Semester One Examinations: 17 November – 6 December 2025 (3 weeks)
- Semester One Holiday Break: 7 December 2025 – 23 January 2026 (7 weeks)
- Semester Two: 10 February – 6 June 2026 (17 weeks) — note: Semester Two's original start date of 24 January 2026 was postponed by the university to 10 February 2026
- 76th Graduation Week: 24–27 February 2026
- Semester Two Examinations: 4–23 May 2026 (3 weeks)
- Recess Term: 6 June – 15 August 2026 (10 weeks)

## How to use this document

- If a student asks "when does semester X start/end" or "when are exams," answer using the year that matches their question and cite this document.
- If asked about an academic year not listed here (e.g., a much older or much newer year than what's shown), state that the information is not available in the current corpus rather than guessing.
- For the authoritative, most up-to-date calendar, students should be directed to https://mak.ac.ug/students/academic-calendar or the Academic Registrar's office.
