# Makerere University — Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BSSE) Programme Overview

**Source:** Makerere University College of Computing & Information Sciences (CoCIS), https://cocis.mak.ac.ug/academics/academic-programs/undergraduate-programs/bachelor-of-science-in-software-engineering/
**Retrieved:** 15 September 2026
**Use:** Reference document for the Student-Support Case Agent's RAG corpus. Content below is a paraphrased summary of the official programme page, not a verbatim reproduction.

## Programme Basics

- **Full name:** Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BSSE)
- **Hosted by:** Department of Networks, School of Computing and Informatics Technology (SCIT), College of Computing and Information Sciences (CoCIS)
- **Duration:** 4 academic years, 8 semesters, including two practical skilling recess terms in Year 1 and Year 2, and a field attachment (industrial training) session in Year 3.
- **Intake:** Offered as both a day programme (mainly government-sponsored students) and an evening programme (private-sponsored students); target enrollment of roughly 100 day + 100 evening students per intake.

## What the Programme Covers

The programme trains students in the principles and techniques of engineering software systems on time, within budget, and with few or no defects — covering design, development, maintenance, testing, and evaluation of software and software-driven systems.

## Career Paths Graduates Are Prepared For

- Software developer / engineer
- Software architect
- Analyst / programmer
- Games developer
- Project manager
- Mobile application developer
- Test analyst / engineer

## Entry Routes

Admission is open to three groups of applicants:
1. Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) holders (direct entry)
2. Diploma holders in a relevant field (e.g., Software Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Electrical/Computer Engineering, Information Technology) from an institution recognized by Uganda's National Council for Higher Education — enter at Year 1 and complete all core and elective courses
3. Degree holders (at least a second-class degree in a related field such as Mathematics, Statistics, or Engineering) via the degree-entry scheme
