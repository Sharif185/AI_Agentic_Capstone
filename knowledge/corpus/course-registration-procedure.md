# Course Registration Procedure (Makerere University)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document explains how a Makerere University student enrols for a semester, registers course units, and pays the fees needed to complete registration. It is written so a student-support agent can answer procedural questions and route unresolved cases to the right office.

It covers: undergraduate freshers and continuing students, retakes, finalists, stay-put students, withdrawal, and a short note on graduate registration.

It does **not** decide anything about admissions, grading, discipline or fee amounts. Those decisions belong to university staff (see section 11).

## 2. Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ACMIS | Academic Management Information System. Powers the student portal, enrolment, registration and invoices. It replaced the older AIMS system. |
| Student portal | Where a student enrols, registers course units, views invoices and generates a PRN. Address: myportal.mak.ac.ug |
| Enrolment | Declaring that you will study in a given semester. Done every semester by the student. |
| Registration | Choosing the course units you will study in that semester. Done by the student in the portal, then confirmed by the Registrar. |
| PRN | Payment Reference Number. Generated in the portal and used to pay an invoice at a bank or by mobile money. |
| Functional fees | Compulsory non-tuition fees, invoiced separately from tuition. |
| Registrar | The College or School Registrar, who verifies documents and completes registration. |
| Stay put | A year spent clearing retakes before continuing normal progress. |
| Semester load | Total credit units (CU) taken in a semester. The maximum is 28 CU. |

## 3. The two parts of registration

1. **College-based registration.** Handled by the College or School Registrar. It is manual: the Registrar checks personal details (for example date of birth and nationality) against your documents. Dates are announced by each College, not centrally.
2. **Self-registration and enrolment.** Done by the student in the portal, once per semester: first enrol, then register course units.

## 4. Registration categories

When registering in the portal, the student picks the category that matches their situation:

| Category | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| Fresher | Newly admitted student registering for the first semester. |
| Continuing student | Student under normal progress. |
| Finalist | Student in the last year of their programme (year 3 of a 3-year programme, year 4 of a 4-year programme). |
| Retakes after final year | Student whose programme period has ended but who still has retakes to complete. |
| Stay put | Student advised not to progress because of low academic performance or too many retakes. |

## 5. Step-by-step: freshers (Year 1, Semester 1)

1. **Account creation.** After admission, the Academic Registrar enters admitted applicants into ACMIS, which creates their accounts. The student activates the portal account (password reset link is sent to the email used in the application).
2. **Enrol.** Log in to the portal, choose the year and semester, select the course units, and enrol.
3. **Pay.** Invoices appear in the portal. Generate a PRN, pay through the bank or mobile money, then allocate the payment to the correct invoice (tuition, functional fees, hall of residence).
4. **Submit bio-data form and documents** to the School Registrar. Bring: the admission letter, birth certificate, O-level results, A-level results, transcripts (for diploma or degree holders), previous institution ID, and proof of payment.
5. **Verification.** The Registrar checks the form against what is in ACMIS.
6. **Registration.** If everything matches and the right course units were chosen, the Registrar registers the student. If there are errors, the student is registered **provisionally** and must fix the errors with the Academic Registrar's office before full registration.

## 6. Step-by-step: continuing students

1. Log in to the student portal.
2. Enrol for the semester and choose the correct registration category (section 4).
3. From the pool of course units, follow the timetabled units and add each one you will study, then submit the registration.
4. Generate the PRN for your invoice(s), pay, and allocate the payment. Receipts are issued through ACMIS only after you have enrolled and registered.
5. Retakes: include the retake course units when you register, but keep the total within the 28 CU semester limit.

## 7. Paying with a PRN

1. Generate the PRN from any ACMIS portal (student portal or the universal payments portal).
2. Pay the PRN at a bank, or with mobile money. On Airtel Money, dial *185# or *165#, then choose Pay Bill, URA, Pay Registered, and enter the PRN. On MTN Mobile Money, dial *165# and use the payments menu.
3. Return to the portal and allocate the payment. If the status does not update, use "Check PRN Status" in the portal.

**Minimum payment before registration.** A 2021/22 freshers' notice required 100% of functional fees and at least 60% of tuition before registration. Treat these figures as the last published values and confirm the 2026/27 rule in the Fee Structure document.

## 8. Deadlines

Registration is mandatory: a candidate is not a bona fide student until registered. The 2021/22 notice set the deadline at the first two weeks of the semester. Exact 2026/27 dates are in the Academic Calendar 2026/2027 and in announcements from each College.

## 9. Special cases

**Withdrawal.** A student may withdraw for at most two periods, each one academic year. Steps: (1) write to the School Board through the Deputy Principal giving reasons and the expected return date; (2) the Board approves or declines; (3) approved students receive a withdrawal letter, with a copy sent to the School Registrar; (4) to return, apply to resume studies (or to extend the withdrawal) and collect the resume or extension letter; (5) the Registrar cancels the withdrawn semester's registration on presentation of the resume letter, the withdrawal letter and the student ID, and the student registers normally. Withdrawal time does not count against the programme's candidature period.

**Stay put.** The Examinations Board advises students whose retakes plus normal courses would exceed 28 CU. A student may stay put at most twice, each for an academic year. Steps: collect a stay-put form from the School Registrar, list the courses and the intended duration, attach the Examinations Board notice, and submit. The Registrar requests the fee amount from the School Bursar, updates the account, and completes registration. The student then generates a PRN and pays.

**Graduate students.** Registration is done at the College or School (MUBS students register at the Directorate of Research and Graduate Training first, then at MUBS). Requirements: evidence of fee payment, two certified copies of academic documents, completed registration forms, a valid ID, and two passport photographs. Graduate students who do not register on time and do not contact the Directorate are treated as having declined their place.

## 10. Common problems and where to send them

| Problem | Route to |
|---|---|
| Cannot log in, forgot password, wrong email on account | DICTS service desk: support.mak.ac.ug (helpme@dicts.mak.ac.ug, 0414 531343 / 437, weekdays 9:00-17:00) |
| PRN not generated, payment not showing, wrong allocation | DICTS service desk first; Bursar's office if it concerns the amount owed |

| Fee amount, instalments, waivers | Bursar's office (human decision) |

## 11. Agent boundaries

- Explain the procedure and point to the right office. Do not promise that a student will be registered, approved, waived or admitted.
- Do not decide admissions, grading, disciplinary or fee matters. Create a support ticket and route it to the responsible office.
- If the question depends on 2026/27 dates or amounts that are not stated here, say so and cite the Academic Calendar or Fee Structure document instead of guessing.
- When creating a ticket, record: student category, the step where the problem occurred, any error message, and the office it is routed to.

## 12. Quick answers

**Is enrolment the same as registration?** No. Enrolment declares your semester. Registration selects course units and is confirmed by the Registrar.

**Do I have to enrol every semester?** Yes.

**Can I register without paying anything?** Registration depends on the minimum payment set by the Bursar's office (see section 7).

**What happens if my documents have errors?** You are registered provisionally and must correct them with the Academic Registrar's office.

**How many credit units can I take?** Up to 28 CU per semester.
