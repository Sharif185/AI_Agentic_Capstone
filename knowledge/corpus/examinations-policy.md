# Makerere University — Examinations Policy

**Source:** Makerere University official website, https://mak.ac.ug/students/examinations
**Retrieved:** 15 September 2026
**Use:** Reference document for the Student-Support Case Agent's RAG corpus. Content below is a paraphrased summary of the official policy, not a verbatim reproduction.

## Eligibility to Sit Exams

- Only students who are registered and have paid required fees may sit University examinations.
- A student must bring their student ID card and/or examination permit. Unclear identification may require extra verification.

## Missed or Deferred Examinations

- A student who misses an exam for a justified reason (e.g., illness, loss of a parent/guardian) should notify their Principal/Dean's office as soon as possible, and no later than the start of the semester in which the exam is scheduled.
- For illness, a University Hospital medical statement is required; other cases need adequate supporting documentation.
- If approved, the student sits a deferred examination when the course is next offered; this is treated as a first attempt, not a retake, and carries a deferred-exam fee.
- If a student is absent without a justifiable reason, they receive a Fail (F) grade for that course, which counts toward their CGPA.
- Religious observance is not, by university policy, grounds for rescheduling an exam — students are expected to inform their Dean/Director in advance if a scheduling conflict with religious practice arises, to avoid an unjustified-absence record.

## Academic Progress Categories

- **Normal Progress:** the student has passed all courses registered for in a semester.
- **Probationary Progress:** CGPA below 2.0 — the student may continue but must retake failed courses.
- **Discontinuation:** three consecutive semesters of probation (CGPA < 2.0), or failing a retaken course a third time, leads to discontinuation from the university.

## Academic Misconduct

Examination malpractice is treated as a serious offence and can lead to expulsion. It includes (non-exhaustively): possessing unauthorized material in the exam room, copying from another student, aiding another candidate, impersonation, obtaining an exam paper before its release, collusion, and plagiarism (presenting another's work or ideas as one's own without proper acknowledgement, in any of its forms — direct copying, uncredited paraphrasing, or stitching together large unattributed sections of another source).

## How to use this document

- Questions about deferred exams, absence policy, or what counts as misconduct can be answered directly from this summary, with a citation to this document.
- Any request to actually defer an exam, appeal a misconduct finding, or make an exception is **out of scope for this agent** — these require human review by the Dean/Principal's office or Academic Registrar, per the project's safety boundary.
- If a student describes a sensitive personal situation (illness, bereavement) tied to a missed exam, the agent should acknowledge it appropriately and route it to a support ticket for human follow-up rather than attempting to resolve it itself.
