# Makerere University — Grading Policy

**Source:** Makerere University official website, https://mak.ac.ug/students/grading
**Retrieved:** 15 September 2026
**Use:** Reference document for the Student-Support Case Agent's RAG corpus. Content below is a paraphrased summary of the official policy, not a verbatim reproduction.

## Marks, Letter Grades, and Grade Points

Each course is marked out of 100 and converted to a letter grade and grade point as follows:

| Marks | Letter Grade | Grade Point | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 90–100 | A+ | 5 | Exceptional |
| 80–89 | A | 5 | Excellent |
| 75–79 | B+ | 4.5 | Very Good |
| 70–74 | B | 4 | Good |
| 65–69 | C+ | 3.5 | Fairly Good |
| 60–64 | C | 3 | Fair |
| 55–59 | D+ | 2.5 | Pass |
| 50–54 | D | 2 | Marginal Pass |
| 45–49 | E | 1.5 | Marginal Fail |
| 40–44 | E- | 1 | Clear Fail |
| Below 40 | F | 0 | Bad Fail |

The Pass Mark for undergraduate courses is 50%. A course credit is only earned once a student scores at least the Pass Mark.

## Degree Classification (Cumulative Grade Point Average)

| Class | CGPA Range |
|---|---|
| First Class | 4.40–5.00 |
| Second Class (Upper Division) | 3.60–4.39 |
| Second Class (Lower Division) | 2.80–3.59 |
| Pass | Below 2.80 (subject to programme-specific rules) |

## Retaking and Improving Grades

- A student who fails a course may retake it the next time it's offered, in order to reach the 50% Pass Mark.
- A student may also retake a passed course to try to improve a low pass grade. If the retake doesn't improve the mark, the original first-sitting result stands on the transcript and is not recorded as a retake.
- Deferred examinations (approved absence due to illness or other justified reasons) are not counted as retakes, since they represent a first attempt.
- Retaking is capped: a student cannot accumulate more than five retake courses at a time.
- Any course that has been retaken is flagged as such on the academic transcript.

## Results and Appeals

- Results are typically published around six weeks after the end of semester examinations, both on notice boards and via the student's university web login.
- Students who believe a mark is incorrect can appeal in writing within 30 days of results being displayed, but only on specific grounds: documented circumstances the examiners weren't aware of, procedural irregularities, or evidence of bias — not simply disagreement with the mark given.

## How to use this document

- If a student asks what grade a given mark corresponds to, or what CGPA a given class requires, answer directly from the tables above and cite this document.
- Grade-change requests, appeals, or disputes about a specific mark are **out of scope for this agent** — these require human review by the relevant department/Academic Registrar, per the project's safety boundary (no grading decisions).
- If asked about something not covered here (e.g., postgraduate grading, which may differ), state that this corpus only covers the undergraduate policy shown above.
