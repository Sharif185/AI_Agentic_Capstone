# ICT Services Guide

*Makerere University | Student portal, email, MakAccess, MUELE, Wi-Fi, PRN payments and IT support*

> **Document type:** Team-created summary (not an official DICTS guide). **Written from:** public pages of the Directorate for ICT Support (DICTS) listed in section 11. **Compiled:** 19 September 2026, for the 2026/2027 corpus of the University Student-Support Case Agent.

## 1. About this document

This guide explains, in one place, the ICT services a Makerere University student uses: the student portal (ACMIS), the university email account, the MakAccess unified portal, MUELE e-learning, campus Wi-Fi, and payment reference numbers (PRNs). It also lists common problems and where to get help. It is written in the team's own words. Details that change often are listed in section 10.

## 3. Activating the student portal (first login)

The student portal at myportal.mak.ac.ug is where a student enrols, registers, generates PRNs and sees results. DICTS describes the first login as follows:

1. Go to https://myportal.mak.ac.ug. The username is the student number. DICTS's first-login instructions give the student number as the initial password as well.
2. The portal then asks the student to add a current email address and phone number. Use details that belong to you, because they are used for two-factor authentication and for online Guild elections.
3. Enter the student number and request a token. The token is sent to the email address you just gave.
4. Use the token to create your own password.
5. Sign in with your student number and the new password. The dashboard shows your profile.

**Invalid username.** This message means the student's data has not yet been moved to the next step, which is loading into the student records module. It is not the student's fault. Log a ticket at https://support.mak.ac.ug.

**Wrong email or phone on record.** Some applicants had the wrong details entered during application. A student whose email is wrong can log a ticket at https://support.mak.ac.ug with the correct email address and phone number so DICTS can update them.

## 4. Student email

The Makerere student email is what gives access to MUELE, MakAIR, Eduroam and library services. A student can create it with the DICTS guide to activating the student webmail account, using the student account option on the single sign-on page (https://sso.mak.ac.ug). Students use webmail at https://webmail.mak.ac.ug with their email address and password.

- **Address format.** DICTS's email page gives the student format as firstname.lastname@students.mak.ac.ug. It also says the university is moving addresses from the unit-based format to username@mak.ac.ug, so the exact format may change.
- **Changing the email password.** This is done in webmail, under preferences and 'Change Mail Password', where the old and new passwords are entered.
- **Reporting an email conflict.** The DICTS help desk has a separate link for email conflict issues.

## 5. MakAccess, MUELE and campus Wi-Fi

**MakAccess.** The Unified Access Portal, announced by DICTS in July 2026, gives staff and students one central entry point at https://access.mak.ac.ug. It provides single sign-on for supported services such as webmail, and lists approved services on a dashboard. Students sign in with their official university credentials, follow any extra authentication steps, and should only use the official link. DICTS says this should reduce the need to remember many separate logins.

**MUELE.** Students no longer follow the old separate process of creating a MUELE account. MUELE is now reached through MakAccess, using the Makerere email account.

**MakAIR and Eduroam.** Students connect with their Makerere email and password. Eduroam is accessed the same way as MakAIR. Follow the DICTS guide to connecting to MakAIR.

## 6. Enrolment, PRNs and paying fees

The portal steps below are summarised from DICTS guides. For the full registration rules, see the Course Registration Procedure document.

- **Enrolment** books the student into the semester and produces an invoice showing the tuition due. Wrong enrolment is corrected by the college registrar.
- **Registration.** First-year students are registered the first time by the college registrar. Continuing students register themselves. Wrong registration can only be corrected by the college registrar.
- **PRN.** Nothing can be paid at a bank or by mobile money until a 13-digit PRN has been generated. The PRN for tuition must be generated in the student portal. A student can generate one for full payment, partial payment, or a deposit to be allocated later.
- **Results.** A student who has not paid tuition cannot view results in the portal.
- **Bio-data.** Students cannot change their bio-data, except the email address and phone number when the portal opens them up. Other changes are made by the college registrar after proof is shown.

### How to pay a PRN:

| Method | Steps published by DICTS |
|---|---|
| Bank | Take the PRN to the bank. Payments are made against the PRN. |
| Airtel Money | Dial *185# or *165#. Choose 4 (Pay Bill), then 7 (URA), then 1 (Pay Registered). Enter the PRN and follow the prompts. |
| MTN Mobile Money | Dial *165#. Choose 4 (Payments), then 5 (Fees and Taxes), then 1 (URA), then 1 (With PRN Payments). Enter the PRN and follow the prompts. |
| Visa or Mastercard | DICTS has a separate guide for card payments through URA after a PRN is generated. |

After paying, the payment should show within a few minutes. If it does not, use 'Check PRN Status' in the portal or https://pay.mak.ac.ug. Only pay through the bank, mobile money or card after generating a PRN. Do not pay anyone who offers to 'help' with fees.

## 7. The ACMIS mobile app

DICTS published a guide in September 2025 for installing the student portal app on Android. The app is called 'ACMIS Student Portal'; the student selects Makerere University as the institution. The guide lists Android 10 or later and about 256 MB of free storage. A student with no account yet taps 'Reset Here!', receives a token at the email they used to apply, and then signs in with the student or registration number and the password they set. The app offers enrolment, registration, results, PRNs, invoices, bio-data and service applications. The guide does not describe an iPhone version.

## 8. Common problems and what to do

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| 'Invalid username' when activating the portal | Student data is not yet loaded. Log a ticket at support.mak.ac.ug. |
| No token email arrives, or email on record is wrong | Log a ticket with the correct email and phone number so DICTS can update the record. |
| Forgot the portal password | Use the forgot-password option and request a token by email. |
| Payment made but not showing | Wait a few minutes, then use Check PRN Status (pay.mak.ac.ug). If it still fails, take the PRN and receipt to the college bursar and log a ticket. |
| Wrong enrolment or registration | See the college registrar. Students cannot fix these themselves. |
| Cannot see results | Check that tuition has been paid, then check with the college registrar. |
| Cannot reach MUELE or MakAIR | Confirm the Makerere email is active, sign in through MakAccess, and follow the MakAIR connection guide. |
| Suspected scam or hacked account | Do not share passwords or tokens. Report to ictsupport@mak.ac.ug. |

## 9. Getting ICT support

- **First contact for ICT problems:** the Senior Assistant Technicians at the student's college.
- **Registration problems:** the college registrar. **Payment problems:** the college bursar.
- **Online service desk:** https://support.mak.ac.ug. A valid Makerere email address is recommended for opening a ticket, and each ticket gets a number to track progress.
- **Email:** ictsupport@mak.ac.ug and helpme@dicts.mak.ac.ug.
- **Phone:** 0414 531343/437 (as printed on the DICTS site). DICTS lists support hours of 9:00am to 5:00pm, and office hours of 8:00am to 5:00pm, Monday to Friday.
- **Walk-in desks:** DICTS, ground floor, Block A, SCIT Building, and DICTS-CEDAT New Building, Level 3.
- **Cost:** a DICTS notice from 2021 told students that ICT support is free and should not be charged for.

### Staying safe online:

- Never share your password or token with anyone, including helpers who offer to fix your portal for money.
- Install antivirus software, keep your operating system updated, and watch for suspicious emails and links.
- Report cybersecurity incidents to ictsupport@mak.ac.ug. DICTS also asks students to read the Computer Misuse Act 2011 and to use social media responsibly.

## 10. Notes for the agent, and details to re-check

- **Never ask for or repeat passwords or tokens.** If a student shares one, tell them to change it and not to share it again. The agent cannot reset accounts. It explains the steps and offers a support ticket.
- **Do not guess account details.** Whether a student's data has been loaded, or a payment has been received, can only be confirmed by DICTS, the registrar or the bursar.
- **MakAccess is new.** It was announced in July 2026, so login steps and the list of integrated services may change. Point students to the official user guide on answers.mak.ac.ug.
- **Email format.** The student address format may change from @students.mak.ac.ug to @mak.ac.ug.
- **Mobile money menus.** The option numbers were published in 2021 and repeated in a 2025 guide. Telecom menus can change, so if a menu does not match, use the bank or the DICTS guide.
- **Contacts and hours.** Phone numbers and hours are as published on the compile date. Confirm before quoting them.
- **Single sign-on address.** The sso.mak.ac.ug advice for creating a student email comes from earlier DICTS posts. Check the current DICTS webmail activation guide.
