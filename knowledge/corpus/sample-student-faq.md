## Courses & Credits

**Q: How many years is the BSSE degree?**  
A: The Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BSSE) is a 4-year, 8-semester programme, with recess terms in Years 1 and 2 and a field attachment in Year 3. *(Source: BSSE Programme Overview)*

**Q: What is the pass mark for undergraduate courses?**  
A: The pass mark is 50%. You must score at least 50 out of 100 to earn the credit for a course. *(Source: Grading Policy)*

**Q: What are the prerequisites for BSE4104 Emerging Trends in Software Engineering?**  
A: BSE4104 requires three prerequisites: BSE3101 Software Architecture and Design, BSE3203 Advanced Software Engineering, and BSE3105 Research Methods in Software Engineering. All three must be passed before enrolling. *(Source: Sample Course Descriptions)*

**Q: How many credit units does BSE4104 have?**  
A: BSE4104 carries 4 credit units, with 3 lecture hours and 2 practical hours per week. *(Source: Sample Course Descriptions)*

**Q: What is BSE4104 about?**  
A: BSE4104 (Emerging Trends in Software Engineering) surveys recent developments reshaping the field. The 2026/2027 syllabus covers AI-assisted development, agentic AI systems, large language models in software products, responsible AI, low-code platforms, WebAssembly, edge computing, and quantum computing fundamentals. Assessment is 60% coursework (research review + proof-of-concept project) and 40% final exam. *(Source: Sample Course Descriptions)*

**Q: What are the prerequisites for the Final Year Project?**  
A: BSE4101 (Final Year Project I) requires completion of BSE3105 Research Methods in Software Engineering and a submitted, reviewed research proposal. BSE4102 (Final Year Project II) requires a pass in BSE4101. *(Source: Sample Course Descriptions)*

## Grading & Results

**Q: What letter grade do I get for a mark of 75?**  
A: A mark of 75–79 earns a B+ (grade point 4.5, interpreted as Very Good). *(Source: Grading Policy)*

**Q: What CGPA do I need for a First Class degree?**  
A: You need a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 4.40–5.00 for a First Class degree. *(Source: Grading Policy)*

**Q: What happens if my CGPA falls below 2.0?**  
A: You are placed on Probationary Progress and may continue your studies but must retake any failed courses. If your CGPA remains below 2.0 for three consecutive semesters, or you fail a retaken course a third time, you will be discontinued from the university. *(Source: Examinations Policy)*

**Q: Can I retake a course to improve my grade?**  
A: Yes. You may retake a course you have passed if you want to improve a low grade. If the retake result is lower than your original mark, the original result stands. Any retaken course is flagged on your academic transcript. You cannot have more than five retake courses running at the same time. *(Source: Grading Policy)*

**Q: When are results published?**  
A: Results are typically published around six weeks after the end of semester examinations, on notice boards and via your university web login. *(Source: Grading Policy)*

**Q: How do I appeal a grade?**  
A: You may submit a written appeal within 30 days of results being published. Appeals are only accepted on specific grounds: documented circumstances the examiners were unaware of, procedural irregularities, or evidence of bias. Disagreement with the mark alone is not grounds for appeal. Grade changes and formal appeals require human review by your department or the Academic Registrar — this agent cannot process them directly. *(Source: Grading Policy)*

## Examinations & Deferrals

**Q: What happens if I miss an exam?**  
A: If you miss an exam without a justified reason, you receive a Fail (F) grade for that course, which counts toward your CGPA. If you have a justified reason (illness, bereavement), notify your Principal/Dean's office as soon as possible and provide supporting documentation (e.g., a University Hospital medical certificate for illness). If approved, you sit a deferred examination when the course is next offered. *(Source: Examinations Policy)*

**Q: Is religious observance accepted as a reason to reschedule an exam?**  
A: No. University policy does not recognise religious observance as grounds for rescheduling. If you anticipate a conflict, inform your Dean/Director in advance to avoid receiving an unjustified-absence record. *(Source: Examinations Policy)*

**Q: What counts as academic misconduct in exams?**  
A: Academic misconduct includes possessing unauthorised materials in the exam room, copying from another student, helping another candidate, impersonation, obtaining an exam paper before its release, collusion, and plagiarism in any form. Misconduct is treated as a serious offence and can lead to expulsion. *(Source: Examinations Policy)*

## Fees Structure

- **Ugandans and East Africans tuition fee:** UGX 1,977,138 per semester
- **International students tuition fee:** UGX 3,840,209 per semester
- **Duration:** 4 years

In addition, the following functional fees apply to all Makerere University students:
- Semester One functional fee: UGX 860,954
- Semester Two functional fee: UGX 132,250

- UNSA Subscription fee: UGX 2,000 per year
- Internship/Field Attachment fee: UGX 100,000 per semester (Year 1, Semester 1 and 2 only)

*(Source: Mak-Undergraduate-Programmes-Fees-Structure-2024-2025AY.pdf)*
